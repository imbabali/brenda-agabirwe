About Catherine

Earning an income is only one part of financial wellbeing

Catherine Brenda Agabirwe is a financial advisory specialist and Financial Literacy Trainer with eight years of professional experience in life insurance sales and financial advisory. She built her expertise in personal financial planning and management, wealth protection and investment at Prudential Assurance Uganda Limited and Old Mutual Uganda.

People also need the knowledge, habits and confidence to make thoughtful decisions about how their income is managed, protected and grown. Catherine helps individuals and groups understand their financial choices and take practical steps towards greater financial stability.

Her training addresses everyday money realities in a practical, engaging and results-oriented way.

The challenges her training addresses

  • Living from salary to salary
  • Struggling with debt
  • Inadequate savings
  • Weak budgeting practices
  • Limited preparation for emergencies and retirement

What Catherine delivers

Financial literacy training services

Practical, engaging and results-oriented training that helps participants understand their money, strengthen everyday financial habits and make informed decisions.

01

Personal money management

Understanding income and expenses, organizing day-to-day finances and developing a realistic approach to managing available money.

02

Budgeting and spending decisions

Building useful budgets, identifying spending priorities and developing habits that help participants take greater control of their cash flow.

03

Savings and financial preparedness

Understanding the importance of saving, setting savings priorities and preparing for unexpected expenses and emergencies.

04

Debt awareness and management

Recognizing the impact of debt, reviewing borrowing decisions and developing more deliberate approaches to managing financial obligations.

05

Financial planning and goal setting

Connecting present-day money decisions to future goals, and planning for important life and financial milestones.

06

Insurance, wealth protection and investment awareness

Building awareness of financial protection and informed investment choices, drawn from professional experience in insurance and financial advisory.
