Beyond saving and borrowing

Her signature session builds a culture of financial wellness for SACCO members. The financial state of a SACCO roots from the individual state of its members, so understanding each member’s personal strengths and weaknesses is what bridges the gaps a SACCO faces.

Just as nature has different seasons, our financial lives also have seasons. Each season comes with different financial opportunities, responsibilities, challenges and priorities.

  1. 1Earning
  2. 2Building

Why the seasons matter

  • They help us prepare for financial challenges.
  • They help us avoid the mid-month financial crunch.
  • They encourage saving and investing during seasons of abundance.
  • They help us make better financial decisions.
  • They reduce financial stress and anxiety.
  • They help us manage debt responsibly.
  • They promote long-term financial stability.

What is your money personality?

The attitudes, beliefs, emotions and behaviors behind how each person relates to money.

The six common money personalities: spender, saver, giver, avoider, investor and planner.

Understanding your money personality helps you:

  • Recognize your financial habits and behaviors.
  • Set financial goals.
  • Understand why you struggle to stick to budgets.
  • Identify emotional and impulsive spending triggers.
  • Develop saving strategies that suit your behavior.
  • Become more intentional about borrowing, spending, insurance and investing.

What type of investor are you?

  • Conservativethe capital preserver
  • Moderatethe balanced planner
  • Aggressivethe wealth-builder
  • Speculativethe active trader

A SACCO that knows where its members lie in personality and financial season can make investment decisions that serve them in each season.

Seven modules, from day-to-day money to investing

Sessions are tailored to each audience and built around the decisions participants face.

  1. 01

    Personal money management

    Understanding income and expenses, organizing day-to-day finances and developing a realistic approach to managing available money.

  2. 02

    Budgeting and spending decisions

    Building useful budgets, identifying spending priorities and developing habits that give participants greater control of their cash flow.

  3. 03

    Savings and financial preparedness

    Understanding the importance of saving, setting savings priorities and preparing for unexpected expenses and emergencies.

  4. 04

    Debt awareness and management

    Recognizing the impact of debt, reviewing borrowing decisions and managing financial obligations more deliberately.

  5. 05

    Financial planning and goal setting

    Connecting present-day money decisions to future goals and planning for important life and financial milestones.

  6. 06

    Insurance, wealth protection and risk management

    Understanding the role of insurance and protection in a financial plan and in managing financial risk, drawn from professional experience in insurance and financial advisory.

  7. 07

    Understanding investments

    Recognizing what kind of investor you are, from capital preserver to active trader, and adjusting the mix of growth, balanced and conservative assets with age, from businesses and land to unit trusts and treasury bonds.

How a session runs

  1. Understand the audience

    Identify participants’ needs, financial questions and the challenges the training should address.

  2. Make the concepts relatable

    Use clear explanations and practical examples to connect financial ideas to everyday decisions.

  3. Focus on application

    Help participants turn learning into action on budgeting, spending, saving, debt and planning.

  4. Build confidence over time

    Reinforce informed decision-making and healthy money habits that last beyond the training.

A career in financial services

Catherine Brenda Agabirwe is a financial advisory specialist and Financial Literacy Trainer. Throughout a career at financial services providers such as Prudential Assurance Uganda Limited and Old Mutual Uganda, she built expertise in personal financial planning and management, wealth protection and investment.

Her training is designed to address these everyday realities in a practical, engaging way.
