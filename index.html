About Catherine

Practical knowledge for confident financial choices

Catherine Brenda Agabirwe is a financial advisory specialist, a Financial Literacy Trainer and a Financial Wellness Advocate. Over eight years in life insurance sales and financial advisory, she built expertise in personal financial planning and management, wealth protection and investment at Prudential Assurance Uganda Limited and Old Mutual Uganda.

People need the knowledge, habits and confidence to decide how their income is managed, protected and grown. Catherine helps individuals and groups understand their financial choices and take practical steps towards greater financial stability.

She is a Senior Partner at Springforth Advisory Limited in Kampala.

The everyday money pressures her training addresses

  • Living from salary to salary
  • Struggling with debt
  • Inadequate savings
  • Weak budgeting practices
  • Limited preparation for emergencies and retirement

Training modules

Financial literacy training, module by module

Practical, engaging and results-oriented sessions that help participants understand their money, strengthen everyday habits and make informed decisions.

  1. 01

    Personal money management

    Understanding income and expenses, organizing day-to-day finances and developing a realistic approach to managing available money.

  2. 02

    Budgeting and spending decisions

    Building useful budgets, identifying spending priorities and developing habits that give participants greater control of their cash flow.

  3. 03

    Savings and financial preparedness

    Understanding the importance of saving, setting savings priorities and preparing for unexpected expenses and emergencies.

  4. 04

    Debt awareness and management

    Recognizing the impact of debt, reviewing borrowing decisions and managing financial obligations more deliberately.

  5. 05

    Financial planning and goal setting

    Connecting present-day money decisions to future goals and planning for important life and financial milestones.

  6. 06

    Insurance, wealth protection and investment awareness

    Building awareness of financial protection and informed investment choices, drawn from professional experience in insurance and financial advisory.

A practical learning approach

From understanding to lasting habits

  1. 1

    Understand the audience

    Identify participants’ needs, financial questions and the challenges the training should address.

  2. 2

    Make the concepts relatable

    Use clear explanations and practical examples to connect financial ideas to everyday decisions.

  3. 3

    Focus on application

    Help participants turn learning into action on budgeting, spending, saving, debt and planning.

  4. 4

    Build confidence over time

    Reinforce informed decision-making and healthy money habits that last beyond the training.
